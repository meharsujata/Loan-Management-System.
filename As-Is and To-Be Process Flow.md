# As-Is and To-Be Process Flow
## Loan Management System

### 1. As-Is Process (Current Situation)

The current-state process is a conceptual business scenario for this portfolio case study, not a verified description of LendingClub's internal workflow.

1. Applicant submits loan information.
2. Loan details are recorded in available records.
3. Applicant and loan information are reviewed.
4. Loan status is updated.
5. Management reviews historical reports separately.

### 2. To-Be Process (Proposed System)

1. Applicant information is captured.
2. A loan record is created with a unique Loan ID.
3. Loan details and verification status are recorded.
4. Authorized users review and update loan information.
5. Loan status is maintained in the system.
6. Portfolio data is consolidated.
7. Dashboards and reports are generated.
8. Management reviews portfolio performance.

### 3. Expected Business Benefits

- Centralized loan information.
- Better visibility into loan records and status.
- Consistent portfolio reporting.
- Easier identification of portfolio trends.
- Improved management reporting.

### 4. Process Limitation

The proposed workflow is a conceptual design. The historical LendingClub dataset does not provide enough information to validate actual application-processing steps or repayment workflows.
