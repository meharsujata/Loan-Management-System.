# Business Requirements Document (BRD)
## Loan Management System (LMS)

### 1. Project Overview
The Loan Management System is a proposed solution to centralize loan and applicant information, improve loan portfolio visibility, and support management reporting.

This portfolio project uses historical LendingClub loan data to identify patterns associated with loan outcomes and translate the findings into business requirements.

### 2. Business Problem
Loan portfolio information needs to be organized and monitored effectively. Without centralized records and structured reporting, it can be difficult for management to review loan performance, understand portfolio trends, and monitor important loan attributes.

### 3. Business Objectives
- Centralize applicant and loan information.
- Improve visibility into loan status and portfolio performance.
- Enable reporting by loan grade, term, purpose, and other relevant attributes.
- Help management identify patterns associated with charged-off loans.
- Support consistent monitoring and informed business decisions.

### 4. Project Scope

**In Scope**
- Applicant and loan record management.
- Loan details and verification status tracking.
- Loan portfolio reporting.
- Loan status monitoring.
- Management dashboards and summary reports.

**Out of Scope**
- Actual loan approval or rejection decisions.
- Live loan disbursement and repayment processing.
- Automated credit scoring.
- Collection and recovery operations.

### 5. Stakeholders
- Business Management
- Loan Operations Team
- Loan Processing Team
- Reporting / MIS Analyst
- IT / Development Team

### 6. Data Source
Historical LendingClub loan dataset containing 396,030 records and 27 columns. The analysis is for portfolio research and does not represent a live lending institution.
